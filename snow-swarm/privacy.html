Privacy Policy

Snow Swarm for Android · Last updated: November 23, 2026

Snow Swarm is a free crowd-runner mobile game. This policy explains the data handled by the game and by its advertising provider. It applies to the Android version of Snow Swarm.

1. Data stored on your device

Snow Swarm saves your level progress, game coins and upgrades, selected skins and colors, language, graphics and sound settings, and acknowledgment of the terms locally on your device. The game does not require an account and does not ask you to provide a name, email address, or password to play. We do not operate a separate player-account server for these save files.

2. Ads and third-party data

Snow Swarm includes Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) for rewarded ads that you choose to view and interstitial ads shown between completed levels. AdMob may automatically collect and share information such as:

  • IP address (which may indicate an approximate location);
  • Android advertising ID and other device or app identifiers, as available;
  • App and advertising interactions, such as ad impressions, taps, and video views;
  • Device, performance, and diagnostic information.

Google uses this information for ad serving and measurement, analytics, security, and fraud prevention, subject to its policies and applicable privacy choices. Information processed by Google is not simply local game-save data. Read Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Mobile Ads data disclosure.

3. Why data is used

Local game data is used to remember your progress and preferences. Ad-related information may be used by Google to deliver, limit and measure advertisements, support advertising and app analytics, and prevent invalid traffic or fraud. The game developer does not independently sell the local game save or request personal identification details.

4. Your choices

Rewarded advertisements are optional. You can adjust advertising personalization or reset/delete your advertising ID where your Android device and Google settings allow it. You can clear the app's storage or uninstall Snow Swarm to remove locally stored game progress. Google's handling and retention of advertising data are controlled by Google's policies and applicable settings.

Where required by law, advertising-related consent choices must be provided through an appropriate consent-management mechanism. Accepting the in-game Terms is not a substitute for any separate advertising or privacy consent required in your region.

5. Data sharing and retention

Local gameplay data is not uploaded to a developer-run account service. AdMob and its partners may process or share advertising-related information according to Google's published policies. Locally stored progress remains until you delete game data or uninstall the game; third-party advertising-data retention follows the relevant provider policies.

6. Children

If you are under the age of digital consent in your region, ask a parent or guardian before using the app. Any special advertising settings required for child-directed or under-age users must be implemented according to applicable law and Google policies.

7. Changes

This policy may change if the game's features, advertising integrations, or applicable obligations change. The updated version and date will be published on this page.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, use the developer-support contact provided in Snow Swarm's Google Play store listing. Local progress can also be deleted directly from Android's app storage settings without contacting us.

This page describes Snow Swarm and its integrated Google AdMob SDK. The information in the Google Play Data safety section should match the app and SDK version actually published.
